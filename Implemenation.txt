| Stage | What we will configure |
|---|---|
| 1. Windows collection -Collect Windows OpenSSH and relevant Windows Security logs 
| 2. Kali collection - Collect Linux authentication and system logs 
| 3. Indexing - Store Windows and Kali logs in separate indexes, such as `windows` and `linux` 
| 4. Analysis - Search logs, extract fields, identify suspicious behaviour, and investigate events 
| 5. Detection - Identify repeated failed logins and other suspicious activity 
| 6. Severity -Assign severity according to detection rules 
| 7. Alerts -Configure scheduled searches, trigger conditions, and alert actions 
| 8. Dashboard - Display events, severity counts, suspicious IPs, and detection results
